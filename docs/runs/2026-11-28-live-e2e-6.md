## Open item 1 — causal recall 33 %: the cause, with numbers

SUP-001 has ~35 purchase orders a week. Weekly late share (seed 42), with the lever's levels:

| Weeks | Jan 1–8 | Jan 15–Feb 5 | Feb 12–26 | Mar 4–18 | Mar 25–Apr 15 | Apr 22–Jun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| late share | 0 %, 0 % | 3–9 % | 11–14 % | 14–25 % | 37–63 % | 34–63 % |
| curve | 0.08 | 0.08 | 0.08 | 0.20 | 0.40 | 0.55 |

- **Not a warm-up artifact.** Order volume is flat from week 1 and `is_late` is a plain bernoulli of a flat
  curve; SUP-002 (3/35) and SUP-003 (7/81) have normal late shares in the first week. January (3.4 %) and
  February (10.4 %) average 6.6 %, consistent with 0.08. SUP-001's two zero weeks are seed luck.
- At 35 orders a week a weekly share has SD ≈ 4.6 pp, so the first phase (+12 pp) is ~2.6 SD per week, and
  noise around 12 Feb already looks like a shift against the zero weeks. Every detective version reads the
  onset as 12 Feb (17 days early, window ±14) and reports the escalation of 25 Mar correctly; the third
  point (+15 pp on 22 Apr) is not distinguishable from the second within the noise.
- Decision: the scoring rules stay; 33 % is the honest score for this dataset. Raising it honestly needs a
  stronger signal at the first point (more volume or a larger first step), not a looser score.
